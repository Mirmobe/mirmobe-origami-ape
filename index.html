Limits of this paper desk
  • DexScreener public data, kept under 300 requests a minute, with a short cache. Scans are about every 20 seconds once the desk is started.
  • GeckoTerminal candles, about 5 calls a minute, cached on this device for 15 minutes.
  • Pollinations text is queued. The on-device model is about 280 MB and needs WebGPU.
  • The desk pauses while the tab is hidden. No fees or slippage are modeled. At most three open paper seats per agent.
  • Replay skips liquidity and the buys/sells ratio. Those numbers are not in the public candles.
